# A Proposal on English Writing Reform - Suppletives
Suppletives are the forms used for irrelatve words to supply inflected forms otherwise lacking.

In the EWR are the suppletives limmited to:
- Conjugations of verb to _be_;
- Infinitives of modal verbs;
- "went" as the past tense of verb to _go_;
- "pence" as the monetary value of _penny_ (_pennies_ for coins).

No longer use _peeple_ (<-_people_) for _persons_.
