# A Proposal on English Writing Reform - Verry special words
Sometimes known as stop-words in data-base monitors.
## Determiners
### Indefinite article - a/an
Write "an" before vowel but "one", "once" and "oneth" (continous letters), "a" elsewhere.
|Before|After
|-|-
|a European|an Europeän
|a union|an union
