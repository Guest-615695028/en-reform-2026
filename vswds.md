# A Proposal on English Writing Reform - Verry special words
Sometimes known as stop-words in data-base monitors.
## Determiners
### Indefinite article - a/an
Write "an" before vocal but "one", "once" and "oneth" (_continous letters_), "a" elsewhere.

|Before|After
|-|-
|a European|an Europeän
|a union|an union

- "An European" is attested at the beginning of the abstract of [_Application of fresh mortar tests to poultices used for the desalination of historical masonry_](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1617/s11527-010-9695-4), from _Materials and Structures_ (fr. _Materiaux et Constructions_).
